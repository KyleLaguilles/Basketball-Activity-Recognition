    gain          = +0.108

    The claim HOLDS for rebound: dense tracks the true ordering more
    closely than the windowed baseline.
    Both rho values reach p < 0.05; the gap between them is still not tested,
    for the reason given in [2].

  Note what a rank result can and cannot say. Rank correlation is invariant to any
  monotone rescaling, so a method can rank perfectly while being badly biased in
  seconds -- that is precisely the case a single correction factor repairs
  (analysis/calibration_factors.py). A method that ranks poorly cannot be repaired
  that way, whatever its mean error. Read this table against [5] of
  analysis/session_duration_error.py, not instead of it.
